Leading engineers is less about having every answer and more about creating conditions where good answers can emerge.

These notes capture what I have learned—and unlearned—over a decade of building software with teams.

## Clarity without control

The best leaders I have worked with set direction without shutting down dissent. They make priorities legible and leave room for people to bring their judgment.

## Care as a practice

Leadership is not a personality trait. It is a set of habits: listening, following through, and telling the truth when something is not working.
