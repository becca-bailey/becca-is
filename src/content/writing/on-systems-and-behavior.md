Software rarely stands alone. It sits inside organizations, markets, and communities—and those contexts change what people do with it.

This essay explores why some platforms encourage collaboration while others amplify conflict, and what designers and engineers might do differently when they take that seriously.

## Systems as environments

Every product creates incentives. The question is whether we notice them before they harden into habits.

When we treat behavior as a side effect rather than a design input, we are surprised by outcomes that were predictable all along.

## What comes next

I am interested in tools that make tradeoffs visible—not just to users, but to the teams building them.
