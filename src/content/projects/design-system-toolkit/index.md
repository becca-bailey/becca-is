Built a design system from the ground up for a growing product team—tokens, documentation, and a component library that met engineers where they already worked.

## The problem

Design and engineering were spending too much time reconciling one-off implementations. Every feature shipped with slightly different spacing, color, and interaction patterns.

## The approach

We started with semantic tokens rather than raw values, documented decisions in plain language, and paired every component with usage guidance—not just props.

## Outcomes

Handoff time dropped significantly, accessibility regressions became easier to catch, and new team members had a shared vocabulary from day one.
