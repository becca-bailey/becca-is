My first large knitting project: a cozy pullover knit top-down with raglan increases and a cable panel down the front.

## Why make things by hand

There is a particular satisfaction in wearing something that took forty hours and three frogged attempts. The flaws are part of the story.

## What I learned

Reading a pattern is its own language. Tension matters. And sometimes the best fix is to walk away and come back tomorrow.
